# Heavy guard art study

`heavy_pose_study.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then edited once to improve pose spacing. Original source has alpha and is preserved without raster post-processing.

Prompt: five front poses of the same original bulky industrial cybernetic guard — idle, stepping, forearm-weapon attack, hit recoil, death — gritty painterly pixel-influenced charcoal/khaki metal with small magenta accents, transparent, no text. Second prompt preserved the design and requested equal cells and no pose overlap.

The resulting sheet is **not** a production 8-direction atlas. Cell spacing is imperfect; only the isolated front idle region `(0,0,424,724)` is consumed by the Enemy Lab's ART STUDY switch. The other poses remain reference material. This switch does not claim temporal animation or directional coverage. Campaign sprites retain their established atlas contract.

In the Enemy Lab: F2 → choose heavy → ART STUDY; compare silhouette, feet baseline and lighting. The sample accompanies authored metal/concrete/neural materials and a worn shotgun vector sample. Review those in the campaign/Material Lab before producing a full asset batch.
